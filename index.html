        Frequently asked questions

        Is Notewell free?+

        Yes. Notewell is completely free to use, with no ads and no subscription.

        Do I need to install anything?+

        No. Notewell works in any modern web browser on your phone, tablet or computer.

        Where are my notes saved?+

        Your notes are saved in your browser on your own device. You can download a backup at any time from your account settings.

        Can I get back something I changed by mistake?+

        Yes. Every time you save, Notewell keeps the earlier version. Open a note’s history to see what changed and bring an older version back.

        What can I attach to a note?+

        Photos and PDF files up to 5 MB each, such as receipts, tickets, warranties or recipes.
